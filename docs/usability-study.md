# OH-pera five-person usability study

Status: prepared, not conducted. Recruit five NYC residents or visitors, including first-time opera attendees and regular opera-goers. Use the same current preview, record the test date and device, and do not coach participants. Do not ask participants to buy tickets.

## Tasks

1. Find a performance you would consider attending this season. Narrow by company and date. Record whether it fits the stated criteria and the time taken.
2. Open the details and explain the story, presenter, venue, date, and time. Identify any unknown or outdated information. Ask: did this help you understand what to expect? Record the answer verbatim.
3. Find where to check the current ticket price and availability, and follow the official ticket link. Stop before checkout. Record whether the destination matches the intended production; distinguish a production page from a preselected performance date.
4. Save the performance, find it in Saved, and remove it. Ask where they expect their saves to be available; explain browser-local storage after the task.
5. Find Sources & updates and explain how an older listing should affect planning.

## Working ticket-information scope

The current implementation sends visitors to the official seller for prices, fees, seat availability, and terms, and links Met listings to official ticket options including rush and student programs. It does not imply a seat is available, quote an unverified price, or imply every date qualifies for an offer. This is an implementation proposal for the PRD's ambiguous “basic ticket information,” not evidence that pricing requirements have been approved or fully met.

## Season convention

“This season” includes future listed dates through August 31 of the current September–August discovery year. Company seasons may differ; the interface states the convention and end date. It does not imply complete season coverage.
